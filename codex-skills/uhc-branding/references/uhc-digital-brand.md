# UHC Digital Brand Reference Notes

Use this file as local orientation only. Current UnitedHealthcare standards should be verified from Brand Center (`https://brand.uhc.com`) whenever network access and authentication allow.

## Source Priority

1. Current Brand Center guidance and user-provided approved excerpts or assets.
2. Existing approved UHC/Abyss design system tokens, components, document templates, or slide masters already present in the target artifact.
3. Publicly available UHC digital design guidance when Brand Center cannot be fully inspected.
4. Conservative accessibility and UI best practices when exact brand guidance is unavailable.

## Working Notes

- Do not claim complete brand compliance unless current Brand Center guidance was accessible and the changed artifact was checked against the relevant pages.
- Do not invent or approximate UHC logos, protected marks, licensed fonts, or proprietary assets.
- Prefer mapping brand guidance into existing theme tokens, component variants, templates, or masters over one-off styling.
- Keep text and UI contrast at WCAG AA or better for normal content unless the approved source system specifies a stronger standard.
- If licensed UHC fonts or approved image assets are missing, use sensible fallbacks and call out the gap in the final response.
